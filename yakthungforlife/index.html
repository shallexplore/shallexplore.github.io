Why this matters

Our history, customs, and identity were carried for generations by voice, by memory, and by the people who lived them. Today, many young Yakthung grow up far from the hills, and far from the stories.

Tracing the Yakthung exists so that never has to mean lost. Through research, writing, and free educational content, we keep our knowledge alive and within reach of anyone who wants to learn it.

Your support keeps this work going, so a young Yakthung anywhere in the world can say, "This is who I am, and I am proud."
